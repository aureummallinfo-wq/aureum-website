Color strategy: The palette is rooted in nature — forest greens, warm golds, and vanilla cream evoke the "arbor" (tree) concept. The unexpected chartreuse accent (#E7FD55) adds modernity and energy to what would otherwise be a purely classical scheme. Dark sections use Pine Green (#2E3A26) at 80% opacity as overlays, never pure black. The Slate blue appears only once, for the "Your Roots" emotional section — a deliberate departure that signals a shift in tone.

05

Animation & Motion

▢
Loading Screen
Centered tree logo on vanilla background. Animated progress bar counts 0% → 100%. Serif percentage counter below. Once loaded, the entire screen fades out to reveal the hero. Duration: ~3–5 seconds depending on asset loading.
✎
Blueprint-to-Render Reveal
Hero opens with an architectural blueprint drawing of the building. As the page loads, the drawing morphs/fades into a photorealistic 3D render. This is the signature animation — it communicates "from vision to reality." Cross-fade with slight scale transition.
Aa
Text Reveal (Character-by-Character)
Large serif headings animate in letter by letter, from semi-transparent to full opacity. Used on the "A NEW CHAPTER IN PRESTIGIOUS LIVING" heading. Each character has a staggered delay (~30ms). CSS: opacity: 0 → 1, transform: translateY(20px) → 0. Triggered by IntersectionObserver.
↕
Parallax Scroll
Full-bleed images scroll at a slower rate than surrounding content, creating depth. Applied to lifestyle photos in the Amenities and View From Above sections. Factor: approximately 0.3–0.5x scroll speed. Also used for the floating text over the aerial view.
↗
Fade-Up on Scroll
Standard reveal pattern for paragraphs, images, and cards. Elements start at opacity: 0; transform: translateY(40px) and ease to visible. Easing: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1). Duration: ~800ms. Triggered at 15% intersection with 60px bottom margin. Respects prefers-reduced-motion.
%
Counter Animation
Numbers count up from 0 to their final value when they enter the viewport. Used for construction progress stats (30%, 10%) and the hero stats bar. Duration: ~1.5s with ease-out. Font: editorialOld serif for the numbers, Work Sans for the labels.
➔
Button Hover
Pill-shaped buttons: on hover, the arrow icon translates right ~4px, and the border subtly lightens. No background fill change — keeps the transparent/outlined aesthetic. Transition: all 0.3s ease. Circle nav arrows: border becomes gold on hover.

Design Principles & Awwwards Patterns

Editorial Storytelling

The page reads like a luxury magazine spread, not a property listing. Each section is a "chapter" with its own mood — dark olive for aspirational, cream for informational, slate blue for emotional. The numbered progress indicator (01–13) reinforces the narrative structure. Content is revealed through scroll, rewarding engagement.

Nature-Inspired Organic Shapes

No rigid grids. Images use blob-like organic masks with large border-radii (20–30px), asymmetric placement, and slight vertical offsets. This mirrors the "arbor" (tree) concept — growth, natural forms, imperfection as beauty. The tree-canopy facade motif from the architecture carries through to the visual language.

Contrast as Currency

The power comes from contrasts: ornate serif vs. clean sans-serif. Dark immersive sections vs. bright informational ones. Massive 96px headings vs. delicate 12px labels. Chartreuse accent vs. muted earth tones. Each contrast is deliberate — the unexpected element (like lime-green on forest green) is what makes it feel premium, not predictable.

Transparent & Minimal Chrome

UI elements are barely there — the header is a floating transparent pill, buttons are outlines not fills, form inputs have only a bottom border. Navigation arrows are thin circles. This "less chrome" approach lets the architecture photography and typography do the heavy lifting. The UI gets out of the way of the content.

Cinematic Scale

Every section uses generous whitespace (120–160px padding), full-viewport images, and oversized typography. Nothing feels cramped. The page is 20,000+ pixels tall — it's designed to be an experience, not a quick scan. The loading screen itself is a deliberate pace-setter: "slow down, this is worth your time." This is the hallmark of Awwwards-winning real estate sites in 2024–2026.
